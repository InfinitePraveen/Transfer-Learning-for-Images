Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our transfer learning application

Transfer learning is a machine learning technique where a model developed for one task is reused as the starting point for a model on a second task. In our case, we use models pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tune them for flower classification.
We offer two models: VGG16 and ResNet50. Both are pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on the Oxford 102 Flower Dataset.
We support JPG, JPEG, and PNG formats. Images should be under 5MB for optimal performance.
VGG16 achieves approximately 85-90% accuracy on the test set, while ResNet50 achieves 90-95% accuracy. The exact accuracy depends on image quality and similarity to training data.
